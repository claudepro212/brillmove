How it works

From username to full review in under 30 seconds.

Enter your username

We load your recent games straight from Chess.com's public game archive.

Pick a game

Stockfish 17 analyzes every position right on your device. No waiting in a queue.

Replay and learn

Step through each move and see what you played, how good it was, and the better move when there was one.

Everything you need to improve

All features are free. No daily limits.

Every move rated

Brilliant, Great, Best, Excellent, Good, Book, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Miss and Blunder, counted for both players.

What you could have played

For every slip, see the better move, why it was better, and the full engine line. Play it out on the board.

Move-by-move replay

Big forward and back controls, keyboard arrows, autoplay, and a jump straight to the key moments.

Accuracy & game rating

Accuracy for each player, an estimated performance rating, and accuracy by opening, middlegame and endgame.

Six free coaches

Pick a coach whose style you like. They comment on each move and can read their advice out loud.

Brilliant Hunter

Scan your last 5, 10 or 20 games at once and collect every brilliant and great move you've played.

Retry your mistakes

Your own mistakes become puzzles. Find the move you missed, with a hint if you need one.

Player insights

Rating trend, best openings as White and Black, longest streak, best win and how your games end.

Coach report

Your coach reads your last 10–30 games and tells you your weakest points, your strengths, your style and what to practice.

Play the coach

Practice against 7 strength levels. Your coach comments on every move and profiles your playing style live.

Link your account

Link your Chess.com username once. Next time you visit after a game, your coach offers to review it with you.

Share cards

Turn a great move or a full game summary into a clean image you can download or copy.
